How a lithophane works

The print is one plate of plastic, thin where your photo is bright and thick where it's dark. Lit from behind, the light shines through the thin parts and the picture appears like magic.

Printing tips

Print standing upright (on its long edge) with a brim, 0.1–0.2 mm layers, 100% infill, light-coloured filament. Relief plates print flat, face up, any infill.

Best photos

Portraits and high-contrast pictures work best. Very dark photos come out thick and muddy — raise the Contrast slider a little and watch the height map.
